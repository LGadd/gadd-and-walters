/ The questions everyone asks"Okay…
what's the catch?"

Fair question. We'd ask it too. Here are the honest answers.

Why is this so much cheaper than everyone else?

Because we're two people who build sites efficiently and host them affordably — not a big agency with offices, sales teams and margins to feed. We'd rather charge a fair one-off price and keep you happy long-term than lock you into expensive monthly contracts.

Do I actually own my website?

Yes. It's built on your own domain with your content. If you ever decide to move on, the site is yours to take. We don't hold anything hostage.

Are changes really free?

Standard changes — new text, new pages, swapped photos, updated opening hours, a seasonal offer — are covered by your £10/month hosting. You email us, we make the change. The only things we quote separately are big new features like card payments or logins.

What's the £10 a month actually for?

It keeps your site online, fast and secure, and it covers our time making your updates. It's not a payment plan for the build — the £500 or £150 is a genuine one-off.

What if I already paid for a site somewhere else?

That's exactly what the £150 redesign and transfer is for. We rebuild it properly, bring your content across, and move it onto your domain — without you starting from zero.

Is there a contract or minimum term?

No long lock-in. You pay the one-off build fee, then hosting month to month. If it's not working for you, you're free to leave.
