About us

Two cousins,
one straightforward idea.

Local businesses shouldn't have to choose between an expensive agency and a fiddly DIY builder. We think there's a fairer way to get a great website — so we built one.

We started Gadd & Walters because we were tired of seeing small businesses pay premium prices for websites they're then expected to build and maintain themselves — or pay again every time they need a change. So we set a new standard: honest, transparent and affordable, with no fees for adjustments or additions.
