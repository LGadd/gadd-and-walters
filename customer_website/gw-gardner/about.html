Established 1966

G & W Gardner was established in 1966 by Mr George Gardner and his son William. Following a successful partnership with William Gardner, the company is now owned by Martin Bedingfield and run by his son Stephen and daughter Julie.

We operate throughout the South East, undertaking the smallest projects through to contracts of approximately £2M. Our work as a principal contractor is long and proven, with a number of repeat clients.

Work is predominantly carried out by our own directly employed workforce, supplemented by specialist sub-contractors with whom we have a long-standing working relationship.
