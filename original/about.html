About the studio

Two cousins,
one straightforward idea.

Local businesses shouldn't have to choose between an eye-watering agency quote and a fiddly DIY builder. We thought there was a fairer way to get a genuinely good website — so we built it, and named it after the two of us.

The G&W GazetteThe people behind itNo. 02

We started Gadd & Walters because we were tired of watching good local businesses overpay to feel stuck.

Between us we handle the whole thing. Laurence designs and builds the sites and is usually the one who replies when you want a change. Mac builds too, and keeps everything running — fast, online, up to date.

There's no call centre, no account manager, no being passed around a department. When you get in touch, you're talking to one of the two people whose name is above the door.

We set ourselves a standard and we stick to it: honest prices printed on the website, quotes up front for anything bigger, and no surprises on the bill. Even when that means a smaller job for us.

We're just getting started — which is exactly why we'll go the extra mile for the businesses who start with us.
