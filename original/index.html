The G&W Gazette Est. 2026 · Kent No. 01

Two cousins, a lot of late nights, and one stubborn idea: local businesses deserve a site that looks like it cost five times what it did.

We grew up making things together, and we kept noticing the same thing — brilliant local businesses stuck with tired websites, because the alternative was an agency quote that made your eyes water, or a DIY builder that fought you every step.

So we started Gadd & Walters. One of us designs and builds; the other keeps everything running and up to date. When you email, a person who actually made your site emails back — usually the same day.

We charge one fair price to build it, a small monthly fee to host it, and nothing for the changes. That's the whole model. No account managers, no lock-in, no clever fine print.

What you're scrolling through is our sample book. Five sections, five completely different styles — proof we can dress your business in whatever suits it best.
